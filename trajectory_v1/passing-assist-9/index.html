One hold, two separate stages

Short Pass depth intent

q = clamp(hold / 350 ms, 0, 1)
D = 8 + 32 × q^1.2 m

Tap → 8 m preferred; half hold → 21.93 m; full hold → 40 m. After selection, the original Short Pass hold-to-arrival-pace system still runs unchanged.

Cross depth intent

q = clamp(hold / 550 ms, 0, 1)
D = 10 + 60 × q² m

Tap → 10 m preferred; half hold → 25 m; full hold → 70 m. This is receiver preference, not the raw manual range. Manual Cross tap remains 20 m. An assisted Cross requests the selected receiver’s distance once, then applies Shot Power capability.

u = perpendicular distance / allowed radius
score = directionWeight × u² + ln(receiver distance / D)²
directionWeight = 3 for Short, 2 for Cross

Lowest score wins among eligible players. Direction cost rises quadratically toward the corridor edge; depth does not grant eligibility. Numerical ties use absolute angle, then stable receiver ID. No player attribute changes receiver selection.

Keep the previous receiver while it remains eligible and its score is within 0.025 of the best score. This is a small, dimensionless switching margin. Manual exit uses the same geometric boundary; there is no unbounded sticky lock. The last preview receiver is recorded at contact, making replay deterministic.
