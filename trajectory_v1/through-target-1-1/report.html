Exact provisional policy

Every 0.15 s, search the authoritative ball’s remaining trajectory through physical rest or the moment the whole ball crosses the pitch boundary. Use the selected runner’s current velocity and heading, sprint capability, acceleration, braking and turn limit. The existing full-sprint movement planner supplies concrete meeting evidence.

Arrival times refer to the candidate future ball state, including availability after it stops. The displayed earliest-arrival estimate is deliberately optimistic: it integrates forward progress at 120 Hz under the existing movement limits, without charging lateral-path error. It is an estimate for filtering hopeless runs, not a guarantee that the runner can execute that path. A concrete feasible meeting inside the useful receive window keeps the chase alive.

REACHABLE: earliest arrival has at least 0.25 s of spare time. MARGINAL: the deficit is no more than 0.65 s, or a concrete moving meeting remains possible. Marginal runners keep sprinting. Larger deficits with no verified meeting are UNREACHABLE. An unfinished forecast remains marginal rather than being treated as proof of failure.

Abandonment: at least 0.60 s initial commitment, four consecutive unreachable assessments and at least 0.45 s of sustained infeasibility. Any reachable or marginal assessment resets this count. Once released, the run cannot reacquire ownership in this experiment.

Useful-through-ball window: physical rest + 1.50 s, with a 0.25 s receive allowance (1.75 s total), anchored to the predicted rest time rather than moving forward on every replan. With no defenders, every inbounds stopped ball can eventually be fetched; this provisional rule separates a useful through-ball opportunity from later loose-ball retrieval. The allowance never extends a pitch exit. Eventual retrieval time is reported separately in the exact diagnostics. This timing policy remains open for owner review.

After release: the runner decelerates along the current velocity at the unchanged Agility braking limit. Position and velocity remain continuous. The ball continues its authoritative simulation. This is the labelled neutral recovery placeholder, not tactical AI.

The standing player’s facing adds no invented startup delay: the unchanged movement controller can accelerate while body yaw aligns. The local pitch is 105 × 68 m: x −20 to 85, z −34 to 34. A ball is out only once its whole 0.11 m radius clears the boundary. Scenario-specific pitch placement is shown in diagnostics.

Scope and limitations

Reachability is a bounded numerical search under the current movement controller, not a proof of a globally optimal run. The neutral recovery placeholder decelerates naturally but does not make a tactical decision. There are no opponents, offside decisions, collisions between players or automatic ownership transfers.

The unchanged RECEIVE continuation uses its original BALL-FEEL-1.3 predictor, while the authoritative ball and pre-contact prediction remain frozen PASSING BALL-FEEL-1.4. This work does not tune receiving technique or the ball.
