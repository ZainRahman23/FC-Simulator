Accuracy formula

First classify the full penalty-area rectangle, then its goal-centred finishing semi-ellipse. Box lines count as inside. The ellipse uses ≤ 1 with a 10⁻¹² dimensionless roundoff guard so computed boundary points count as inside. No random zone selection or rating rounding is used.
Independent triangular errors are centred on the ideal launch. Total speed and the resolved spin vector stay unchanged; only launch yaw and elevation vary in this separate visualization.
Projection, not outcome sampling
Angular density is transformed into spatial density with the projection’s Jacobian. Contours map the highest-density angular regions; their enclosed probability remains exact mathematically, subject to the stated drawing interpolation.
The normal hold is the same frozen pre-contact solution as the approved visualizer. The zone marker does not re-solve this shot. This introduces no zone classification or accuracy code into real shots.
