# Cagey / Open Chance Ecology — v0.7

"Cagey" is not synonymous with sterile football, and "aggressive" is not synonymous with automatic two-way chaos.

A competent controlled team can use patient buildup, regrouping and a connected mid/narrow block while still assigning a creator, runner, wide runner and/or run-behind threat to penetrate. Controlled football should reduce transition risk and chance volume while retaining enough attacking quality to create and score.

The ultra-low preset is intentionally different: deep/passive/drop defense, secure progression, cautious box commitment and very low forward effort. Ultra-v-ultra is therefore the environment where extremely low total xG should cluster, especially with weak attackers.

Matchup interaction is the key:

- Ultra vs Ultra -> very low xG.
- Good attack vs Ultra -> territorial siege and meaningfully more attacking xG, but fewer two-way transitions.
- Aggressive vs Ultra -> pressure against a set shell; the low block's counter commitment determines how open the match becomes.
- Aggressive vs Aggressive -> the strongest two-way transition ecology.

None of these labels directly modifies shot xG or scoring probability. They alter positions, action choice, number of runners, turnover risk, defensive organization, receiving geometry, pressure and workload.
