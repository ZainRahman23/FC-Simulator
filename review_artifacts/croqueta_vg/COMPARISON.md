## Why the current croqueta does not look like the reference

It is **two foot touches underneath a running body**:

- **No set-up and no load.** The body never decelerates, never lowers, never widens its base. It arrives at the touch in an ordinary jog.
- **The pelvis is still on the entry line at the touch** (0.03 – 0.10 m across). In the reference it is already 0.2 m across: the body leads the ball.
- **The ball leads the body.** At the receive the ball is 0.20 m further across than the pelvis. The eye sees the ball being moved and the body catching up.
- **The trunk stays upright with running arms**: pitch 6 – 10°, against ≈ 35 – 40°. Nothing in the upper body says "lateral transfer".
- **The second touch is a separate action** (GATHER), and the receiving foot is mid-stride at the first touch. The two touches read as touch → step → touch.
- **The speed is constant.** Without the reference's stop-and-go rhythm (slow set-up, sharp shift, re-acceleration), the move has no punctuation.

Good, and kept: the inside surfaces, and the receiving foot ending beyond the ball. The walk's touch-to-touch interval (0.30 s) is already on the reference's rhythm.
