## The body–ball relationship

The owner's hypothesis holds, with one correction. The video's real overlapping sequence is:

1. **The body loads**, low and wide, with the ball inside the left foot.
2. **The pelvis and centre of mass start toward the right foot before the touch** (≈ −0.10 s). At the touch they are already ≈ 0.2 m across: the body leads.
3. **The left inside rolls the ball across under the moving body**, the left leg long as the push-off.
4. **The left leg organises underneath**: its follow-through brings it down beside the right foot.
5. **The right foot steps out and meets the ball beyond the body's line** (+0.28 s).
6. **The body continues into the new lane**, and the same foot keeps the ball.

The correction is to the order. **The pelvis is already moving before the first contact.** It does not follow the touch.
