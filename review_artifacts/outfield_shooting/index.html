Touchline — Shooting V1

Five strike families on the skeletal rig, driven by the shooting model that was already in the simulation.

Recovered, then migrated. The simulation already owned shooting: ptKick, the technique table, the launch families, foot selection, the charge curves, and the sprite library whose frame counts define the authoritative contact instant. None of that was redesigned. The five requested shots map onto existing techniques with no new simulation concepts, and each authored action carries the rhythm of its recovered sprite entry. Animation ON and OFF produce bit-identical shots on every family, both feet, standing and on the run.

Known limitations

  • Dribbling V1 roulette. Carried over and recorded, not fixed here. On a hard reversal the back-pedal flag flips twice and the leg basis target jumps between the movement direction and the facing, roughly 180 degrees apart. The basis then slews the long way round at its cap of 516 deg/s, sweeping half a turn in about 0.35 s, which reads as an unintended spin. It does not interfere with shot entry, so it is left for the dribbling polish pass.
  • First-time strikes compress to the bound. The simulation's first-time branch gives 0.2 s of approach; Power's natural approach is 0.47 s, so the load is entered already in progress. Reported as a clamped warp rather than hidden.
  • Left-footed Power borrows another schedule. The recovered sprite library has no dedicated left Power set, so the simulation falls back to the left LACES timing. The action still plays as Power, time-warped onto that schedule.
  • No attribute differentiation yet. The clean inputs are identified — execution precision, contact quality, power, placement, spin, weak-foot execution, consistency under body state — and preferred foot is already honoured, but nothing is tuned and there is no rating term.
  • Scope. No volleys, headers, bicycle kicks, skill shots, set pieces, defender pressure or keeper redesign.
