2 · The model

EXECUTION (per axis a of the surface's frame: rebound / lateral / third)
  v_out = v_ideal + Σ_a  σ_a · N_a            N_a ~ N(0,1), keyed: seed | RECEIVE | receiver | receive event | receive-kill-v1 | axis | 0   (no Ball Control in the key)
  σ_a   = k_a,surface · vRef · D · E(BC, D)    vRef = 10 m/s
  E     = eFloor + eSkill · q(BC) · c(D)       eFloor 0.0068 · eSkill 0.4
  q(BC) = ((100 − BC) / 90)^1.1            1 at BC 10 … 0.0071 at BC 99
  c(D)  = D / (D + 1)                     Ball Control's share of the error: 0.17 at D 0.2, 0.50 at D 1, 0.75 at D 3
  rebound N < 0 (under-cushioned, the ball runs through) is scaled by the surface's run factor; a foot on the turf can only lift the ball (|N_third|)
  caps: rebound along n ≤ max(ideal, e · v_in,n) (an uncushioned bounce off that surface); run-through ≥ −0.9 · v_in,n
DIFFICULTY
  vEff = sqrt((w_h · v_h)² + (w_z · v_z)²)    the square root of the weighted incoming kinetic energy per unit mass
  D    = m · (vEff / vRef) · (1 + 0.6·H² + 0.8·J + 0.5·B + 0.1·shift)
  H = (z − zNom)/zHalf (±1.5) · J = (z − 1.92)/0.3 for a header above 1.92 m · B = (1 − cos bearing)/2 · shift = repositioning (m)

7 · Limitations and decisions

  • The body is a procedural placeholder (the contract: RECEIVE_KILL1_ANIMATION_CONTRACT.md); it is not in the ball's world, so a run-through ball passes through the legs.
  • The receiver is stationary in clean conditions: no opponent, pressure, fatigue, weak foot, Agility or Dribbling (the owner's isolation). Both feet are allowed with no weak-foot term; AUTO prefers the right foot only as a tie-break.
  • One contact only: the kill's result is the free ball; the follow-up touch (e.g. the foot after a chest control) is not modelled yet.
  • The constants are calibration values, not locked: eFloor, eSkill, γ, Dc, the surface weights, capabilities and error shapes.
