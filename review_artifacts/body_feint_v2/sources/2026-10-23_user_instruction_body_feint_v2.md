# CENTRAL PRINCIPLE

A BODY FEINT IS NOT:

```text
torso rotates left
→ player goes right
```

and it is not:

```text
small shoulder animation
+ ordinary cut.
```

A convincing football body feint is a coordinated false movement involving the player's entire movement system.

The defender should plausibly read:

> **"This player's body is about to go THERE."**

before the player exits somewhere else.

---

# THE WHOLE BODY MUST SELL THE FAKE

Study and author coordinated behavior across:

```text
HEAD / GAZE where appropriate

SHOULDERS

CLAVICLES

ARMS

CHEST

CENTRE-OF-MASS SHIFT

SUPPORT LEG

FREE LEG

FEET

STEP WIDTH

FOOT ORIENTATION

BODY LEAN
```

Every part does not need exaggerated movement.

In fact, subtle coordination is preferable.

But the final action must read as **one human body committing information in the fake direction**, not independent joints playing little animations.

# MESSI REFERENCE CHARACTER

Messi is particularly useful because many of his best feints are not flashy skill moves.

The deception often comes from combinations such as:

```text
small approach
slight deceleration
support loading
hip suggestion
shoulder/chest lean
arm counterbalance
tiny foot/ball cue
explosive opposite exit
```

The fake can be extremely economical.

Do NOT conclude that a stronger feint requires a larger animation.

A very good feint may be:

```text
small
fast
coherent
well timed
```

rather than theatrical.

# THE FEINT MUST EXIST IN THE SUPPORT SYSTEM

This is critical.

Do not create a cosmetic upper-body feint over unchanged locomotion.

If the player sells movement left, there should be an appropriate real change in:

```text
support loading
pelvis position
body lean
step/foot preparation
```

that makes the false direction believable.

But the fake should stop short of becoming such a huge committed movement that the player cannot exit efficiently.

This is the technical skill:

> **Show enough genuine body commitment to sell the false direction while retaining the physical ability to explode out the other way.**
