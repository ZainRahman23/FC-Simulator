**V1** is the committed `croqvg/` build (4a549b8f), run untouched. All measurements are from the same case: the right foot's ball, the request 40° left, a jog (`REF · JOG R → L`), with t = 0 at the first touch.

## Why V1 read as lateral stepping

V1 was built from a walking croqueta. A walking player can plant the receiving foot wide early and move the body over it. At a jog that becomes three readable steps:

1. **Plant wide.** The receiving foot goes down on the exit side before the touch.
2. **Shift.** The body travels across over it: 0.24 m before the touch, 0.71 m in all.
3. **Step out.** The receiving foot lifts again and steps to the ball.

The lateral motion is spread over the whole sequence, peaks late (+0.31 s), and has no single impulse. With the ball hidden, the body reads as **sideways steps**.

The new reference has **one** impulse. The ball-side foot sweeps the ball and immediately plants under the body to push, while the receiving foot is already in the air, hopping across to land on the ball. That single event is what makes it a croqueta.
