## The support sequence (rear-low clip; t = 0 at the first touch, ≈ 3.50 s)

| t (s) | What happens |
|---|---|
| −0.10 … 0 | **Start shape:** the **right leg is out to the right with the ball** at its inside; the **left foot supports under the body**. The owner's "/\|". |
| 0 | **The right inside sweeps the ball across**, and the right foot travels with the ball. |
| ≈ +0.03 … +0.05 | **The left foot unweights**: it lifts off. |
| ≈ +0.08 … +0.10 | **The right foot comes down under the body**, near where the left foot was, and **pushes the body left**. Its leg then extends long behind-right as the push-off. |
| ≈ +0.10 … +0.15 | The left foot flies across to the left with the body: **the hop**. |
| **≈ +0.15 … +0.20** | **The left foot meets the ball** at its inside, beyond the body's line, as it lands. |
| +0.20 … | **End shape:** the left leg out in the new lane with the ball, the right leg long behind. **The legs have changed sides** (the owner's "\|\\"). Then GO: the left foot keeps the ball. |

**Touch to touch is ≈ 0.15 – 0.20 s**, much quicker than the walking reference's 0.28 s. The lateral impulse comes from **the ball-side foot after its sweep**, not from a step across. The receiving foot is in the air for most of the transfer: it does not step out from a planted position.
