Why S* fails: the elastic reserve of each axis at entry

The fixed excitation is the rest statics divided by capacity, plus 0.02 co-contraction. A side that carries only the co-contraction has a strain reserve (e − 1)·0.02·capIso of 0.15–1 N·m. The hip, lumbar lateral and knee therefore have sub-degree elastic ranges. The hip's 17 N·m/rad compares with a human 48–74 per side (Kiemel 2008), and the body sits back into a squat open-loop with e-folding 0.125 s (λτ = 1.4).
