WHAT K AUTHORS
Gabriel enters from a short compatible moving stance. The right thigh drives,
the left leg opens back/down, the trunk opens and rolls, then the right knee
extends as the pelvis descends. The left leg folds later into the side slide.
The head follows the challenge and the arms counterbalance, briefly brace,
and settle. Forward motion continues throughout the landing and follow-through.

This is a rotation-only pose stream on the exact 23-bone Gabriel bind, plus a
separate authored root trajectory. No limb lengths or mesh proportions change.
Scalar authoring curves use C1 PCHIP interpolation. There is no explicit setup
hold, transition blend, or phase-local root velocity reset. The entry is not a
RUN-1 transition, and does not claim continuity with an unspecified live run.

AUTHORED GROUND SEQUENCE
The left boot approaches first and comes within 16 mm of the turf at 0.383 s.
The outer left thigh comes within 16 mm at 0.550 s. This surface carries the
visual landing while the pelvis rolls above it. The left forearm/hand comes
within 16 mm at 0.608 s and then releases during the established slide.

These timings use dominant-bone surface groups and geometric proximity, not
simulated force. The pelvis-weighted mesh region remains about 78 mm clear,
and the torso-weighted region about 154 mm clear. The leading boot remains
raised (minimum approximately 156 mm after commitment). This is a left-thigh
and side slide, not a claim that every named region physically contacts turf.

The root height contains an offline smooth clearance bake against the complete
skinned mesh. The lowest sampled surface is +4 mm at 120 Hz. Every vertex also stays inside the final detail crop, with at least
39.7 pixels of bottom margin at 120 Hz. No triangles are
hidden below the ground, no limb is shortened, and there is no runtime contact
solver. Body-to-body collision and physically valid loads are later work.

PRESENTATION
Frozen, unmodified Gabriel rig.json / mesh.bin / atlas.png and the actual
Touchline of_character / of_char_gl / gl_renderer code render the character.
The accepted CAMERA_V1 uses 30 m height, 43 m behind the touchline, 22-degree
pitch and 28-degree vertical FOV, zoom 1. Native review preserves 1280x720
framing and a fixed rail. The labelled 10x detail view re-renders a crop of the
same projection at higher resolution, following the root horizontally.

The field uses Touchline's actual grass tile palette, exact copied ground
texture functions, mowing boundaries, and a projection port of the pitch
markings. It is an isolated field presentation, not a running match. Stadium
props and other players are outside this harness. The planar shadow is a
presentation aid and exerts no force.

The secondary low three-quarter view is approximate and uncalibrated. It
does not replace the primary gameplay camera or act as a pose-fitting target.

VISUAL ITERATION
The full motion was reviewed through normal-speed browser playback and
sampled screenshots. Internal candidates were rejected for a boot-supported
high landing, an overly upright high-kick opening, and an early bracing hand
that held the torso above the landing. The final candidate coordinates the
pelvis, torso, both legs and arms; it is not another isolated shin correction.
The earlier exact-landmark optimization is not called or imported by K.

VALIDATION AND LIMITS

quaternion continuity, positive forward travel, asset identity, geometric

review; they do not declare the visual gate passed or prove biomechanics.
