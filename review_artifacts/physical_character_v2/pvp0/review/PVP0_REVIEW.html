5. The three largest visual problems

1 · The tackler does not perform a slide tackle

It lands hard (−4.86 m/s vertical at the instant of contact), rolls face-down into a belly-flop with the arms forward and the left leg up, and stops within about 1 s. K authors a side-on slide. CALIB proves this is not contact: the tackler alone does the same. Two upstream causes feed it. The retarget places V2's authored support foot about 13 cm above the turf where Gabriel's toe touches, so K's slowed descent becomes a free fall from 3.2 m/s. And the start had to be airborne at t 0.30, because K's run-up is not physically executable.

Owner: TACKLER PHYSICAL TRACKING (joint-space tracking cannot hold whole-body orientation, and there is no root drive by rule). Contributing: INTEGRATION (retarget vertical anchoring) and TACKLER ACTION (K's run-up and flight; K's Gate A is still open).

2 · The bodies lock together after contact

The tackler's tracker keeps driving the tackling leg toward K's target after the strike, so the boot stays pressed into the shin and foot (260–500 N for over 3 s in LIGHT). The recipient can neither free the foot nor step. Result: LIGHT stands pinned like a statue; NOMINAL's trapped foot makes him sit down on the tackler; HEAVY is lifted by the tackler's upturned left leg (522 N·s pelvis × shank_L) and flipped.

Owner: TACKLER PHYSICAL TRACKING (constant authority after contact: the leg deflects locally but the intent never releases). Contributing: RECIPIENT BALANCE (C3 has no step).

3 · The recipient reads as a mannequin

No step, no arm response, no hop over the boot. The struck ankle is held at about 34° for over 3 s. Under light contact the only visible response is a lean. The mechanics underneath are right (yield, ordered propagation, load shift), but nothing shows them.

Owner: RECIPIENT BALANCE (C3 is a no-step, arms-passive baseline by design; the shortfall is behavioural, not a contact-physics fault).

Also: at the actual gameplay camera (zoom 1) the event is too small to read (RENDERING; existing camera, not a regression).

10. Method

  • World: the frozen PVP-1A two-character plant: one Jolt world, all cross-character pairs colliding, per-manifold contact decode, per-character and system momentum / energy ledgers, and the equal-and-opposite check. Two law hooks, each through its character's canonical M1b body.
  • Recipient: PPR-3 standing entry, C3 active from step 0, stationary. Tackler parked kinematic and inert until step 408 (1.7 s). Then dynamic, with K's own body velocities at t 0.3 s plus (s − 1) × K's horizontal COM velocity. One velocity write per body is its initial condition. No pose writes after construction, no root drive, no external force, no ragdoll switch, no outcome logic.
  • Tackler intent (KTracker): per actuated axis, K·err + D·(ω_ref − ω) − τ_static toward K's joint targets (240 Hz slerp), using V2's own PostureDriver gains. This is Astra's authority model A, "constant finite". The intent becomes torque only through M1b: activation lag, capacity, force–velocity and strain-arm eccentric yield.
  • Retarget: V2 body rotation = Gabriel bone rotation · C (arms ∓90° about z). V2 forward kinematics from Gabriel's hip mid-point. Joint targets qcs(parent, child), not clamped.
  • Placement: From CALIB (NOMINAL speed, tackler alone): 0.22 s after activation the right boot (COM) is at 0.20 m height, just after landing, moving −z at ≈ 5 m/s. Target: the boot passes through the recipient's LEFT shank axis (x −0.03, z +0.21 local, the lower shin) at t_c = 0.22 s. Lateral offset x = 0.29 m (the boot runs 0.32 m to the −x side of the K pose offset). Along the line of travel: boot displacement over t_c = s·ΔzCOM + (Δz_boot − ΔzCOM) with ΔzCOM = −1.43 m and Δz_boot − ΔzCOM = −0.07 m measured in CALIB; the horizontal COM motion is ballistic until landing (≈ 0.19 s), so the start moves back by 1.43 m per unit speed factor: offset z = 2.42 + 1.43·s. The same rule for every strength; nothing tuned per case.
  • Why t 0.30: K clip time of the tackler's start: the airborne scissor (lowest V2 point ≈ 0.17 m above the turf; K COM 6.7 m/s forward, 1.05 m high). K's authored run-up accelerates 2.5 → 7 m/s in 0.25 s (≈ 1.8 g from one stance leg), which no physical body can reproduce; the physical tackler therefore starts in the air at K's own dive state and receives NO root propulsion
  • Twin / CALIB: TWIN = the same world with the tackler parked for the whole run (the recipient's undisturbed reference). CALIB = the NOMINAL tackler 10 m clear (the no-contact tackler reference, and the placement measurement).
  • Not modified: C3, M1b, RUN-1, simulation collision decisions, authoritative outcomes and gameplay tackle logic. B3 is not used.
