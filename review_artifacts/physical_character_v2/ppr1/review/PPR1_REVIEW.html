Why CURRENT V2 reads stiff (audit)

  • Zero-latency torque generation. The actuator's excitation is computed from the same step's pose-spring demand. In the collision: activation 9 % → 96 % in ≈ 50 ms, torque rates 12,700–16,500 N·m/s from 0 ms, and up to 68 N·m per axis beyond what the entry activation could give.
  • About 100× human damping. The ankle sums to 349 N·m·s/rad (D / K 0.35 s). At 50 ms after a push, 78–97 % of the resistance is viscous.
  • Stiffness that never yields. 1.5 × the supported load on every joint and every axis (ankle 1.54 mgh vs human 0.64–0.94), around a frozen whole-body pose.
  • Not causes: frozen gravity compensation inside the window; the passive tissue layer.
