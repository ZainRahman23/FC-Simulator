IFM-1 · slow-motion collision review (C0 | N)

This is a visualisation of the committed IFM-1 records only. Nothing was re-simulated, tuned, amplified or interpolated. No visual-value verdict is made here; the preregistered labels below are shown exactly as recorded, and every cell is still VISUAL_PENDING.
Each video: LEFT = C0, the no-hit control. RIGHT = N, native contact. Both sides are one composite frame, so they are always locked to the same instant.
Content: 100 ms of frozen RUN-1 lead-in, then entry into V2 at τ = 0, then every recorded 1/240 s step to 150 ms. Nothing is synthesised between steps.
Camera: the frozen RUN-1 viewer and character, the gameplay camera CAMERA_V1 with the viewer's own zoom at 8×. That is the same camera pose and perspective, an exact 8× magnified crop of the 1280×720 gameplay projection.
Speed: 0.25× shows one step per frame at 60 fps. 0.1× shows one step per frame at 24 fps.
Versions: CLEAN hides the diagnostic impactor after the contact step. DIAGNOSTIC draws overlays on the exact same character frames.
Verification: SLOWMO_VERIFICATION.md.

Questions this aid is for. They are for you to answer; nothing here answers them.

  • Does motion first diverge at the struck body part?
  • Does the disturbance propagate naturally through connected joints?
  • Do remote body parts initially preserve inertia?
  • Does a planted foot or support state provide plausible resistance?
  • Does the response look articulated rather than like whole-body translation?
  • For PL and TP: can you see the effect of continued native contact? The contact steps are marked yellow under the timeline.
  • Is any apparent response actually present in C0 as well?
