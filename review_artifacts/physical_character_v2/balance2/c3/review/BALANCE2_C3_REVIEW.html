BALANCE-2 C3 review: balance within the rigid foot's legitimate support

RECIPIENT STATUS: C. BODY / STANCE ARCHITECTURE DECISION REQUIRED (by the frozen C3 rule)

Owner decision (12 Oct, 22:12): rigid boot-tip support is not legitimate. C3 = C2 + a support-envelope governor: the AP command is bounded so that the modelled developed force stays inside C2's own flat-foot envelope (heel edge to the oblique MTP line). No gain, anatomy, stance, foot, criterion or fall-rule change.

  • Legitimate support: yes. L1 pass: no recovered run in the whole matched battery loads the boot tip (B and C2 both fail this). L2 pass: physical forward limit 12.13 N·s = frozen-rule threshold 12.09 N·s, so beyond it C3 genuinely falls.
  • Causality: PASS (C1–C4; the governor first reacts at steps 499 / 505 = onset + 1 + the hip / ankle COM-channel delays).
  • Matched battery: 15 / 17, failing P8 and T1. P8: backward small overshoot 0.287 (≤ 0.26; unchanged from C2). T1′ forward 12.09 N·s, below the band's 13.01.
  • Why C, not B: the T1′ forward band is scaled to the battery edge (102.95 mm), which the rigid boot reaches only by pitching onto its tip. Against the legitimate flat-foot loaded edge (80.3 mm), C3's threshold is 0.595 of the zero-delay limit, inside the human "try not to step" range 0.50–0.70. B's (0.708) and C2's (0.82) in-band forward thresholds used the boot tip.

Not frozen as the qualified recipient baseline (that needs 17 / 17). The decision is the owner's: articulated toes, stepping, or a band derived at the legitimate edge. PVP was not run.
