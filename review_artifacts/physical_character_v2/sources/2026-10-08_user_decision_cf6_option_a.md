Decision: choose (a), with (c) permitted to emerge naturally. Do not implement (b).
Keep the pelvis/balance foundation exactly as V2 currently defines it. CF-6 is specifically testing whether the existing V2 physical character can support recognisable walking once the missing gait-level planning, unloading, heading/stance regulation and walking swing mechanisms are supplied. Adding deliberate pelvis-height motion now would change that question too substantially.
Use shorter steps/higher cadence where required by V2's existing reachable envelope. Do not force human step lengths that the fixed-height V2 body cannot physically reach. If heel rise/toe rotation occurs physically through the existing rigid-foot/contact/joint system, allow it and measure it, but do not prescribe or add a new heel/toe mechanism merely to reach a target.
Treat the ~0.3 m flat-foot reach ceiling as an observed V2 constraint, not automatically as V3 evidence. If that ceiling prevents normal human walking speeds even after walking-aware planning and cadence are used, classify the immediate cause precisely. Missing gait functionality is A; a correctable controller limitation is B; only an unavoidable limitation of the underlying body/joints/actuators/contact model is C.
Do not freeze or implement CF-6 yet. Wait for the external research package for items 1–4. Once supplied, cite it in the preregistration, define the speed/cadence ladder from it, freeze the preregistration, and proceed exactly under the previously approved CF-6 protocol.
