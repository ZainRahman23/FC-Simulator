## 2. Causal diagnosis (post-hoc; `evidence_td2c/diagnostics/u1_diagnosis.txt`)

**1. The excess is entirely at the +20 mm collision.**
- Worst case (obs20 V2-190-85 L 180 C-F7, Σ+ 0.771 J vs 0.164 J for the same case's nominal run): 0.537 J of positive closure lies in the 50 ms collision window, 0.107 J just after it, and the remaining phases match nominal.
- Σ+ scales with the timestep in every condition (nominal medians 0.154 / 0.108 / 0.069 J; obs20 0.383 / 0.295 / 0.207 J at 180 / 240 / 480 Hz).

**2. No net energy is generated by the collision; the window starts one tick late.** The first contact force appears one solver tick **before** the sensed contact onset:
- at 8.9278 s, Fz 555 N, closure −0.501 J, while `touch` is 0;
- onset registers at 8.9333 s.

U-1's frozen window (amendment A2.8, "contact-onset tick (touch 0 → > 0)") therefore starts **after** the impact's first dissipation tick. Measured from that tick:
- the collision's cumulative closure never rises above its pre-impact value (maximum −0.140 J);
- its net is −0.352 J.

The 18 part-(c) failures are this one-tick offset at 180 Hz.

**3. The positive closure ticks come with IK-derived feed-forward active during the impact velocity jump.** Counterfactuals on the same case:
- **No IK-derived feed-forward (R0):** Σ+ 0.144 J; window positive closure 0.014 J. R0 also changes the swing (later liftoff, contact at φ 0.87), so this is indicative.
- **Guard off (TD2B):** Σ+ **0.984 J**; window positive 0.458 J. No blow-up in this case.
- **DVG (frozen configuration):** Σ+ 0.771 J.

So **DVG reduces Σ+ against guard-off**; it is not the source.

**Reading:** an energy-ledger closure error at a stiff impact, proportional to the timestep and to the actuator torque held across the impact (the feed-forward is still commanded on the impact tick, then held during the guard's fade). The ledger books motor work per tick as torque × joint velocity across a velocity discontinuity. It converges with rate (480 Hz: 0 failures) and is the same class of timestep-dependent integration error as TD-15. **Not established:** an independent fine-time-step work integral was not computed.

## 4. Decisions needed (none taken)

1. **U-1 for the out-of-envelope +20 mm obstacle at 180 / 240 Hz.** The frozen U-1 fails, and redefining it after the result would be a criterion change. Options:
   - **(a)** keep U-1 as frozen; TD2C stays FAIL, and the 180 / 240 Hz impact closure is investigated as integration debt (for example an impact-aware work ledger, or the TD-15 line);
   - **(b)** a versioned U-1:
     - start the collision window at the first contact-force tick, not the sensed onset (fixes part (c));
     - judge collisions by net non-generation, with Σ+ reported, or attribute Σ+ excess to TD-15 by a preregistered rule like CQ-5's;
     - then re-evaluate on these runs;
   - **(c)** a controller change at impact, e.g. suppress the IK-derived feed-forward from the first measured contact force. That is new mechanism work.
2. **Then, if TD2C passes,** the prepared path is ready:
   - E2 integration: the patch is verified, default-path identity holds, and the integrated step runs end to end;
   - SV-2R, which would validate, followed by entering the allowance;
   - PG-1, then the prerequisites, then official E2.
3. **Known risk for official E2** (declared non-test smoke SMK-R on the final configuration, diagnostic only):
   - the R-B recovery steps still fail R-4 (impact 54 % BW, tangential approach 0.15 m/s) and R-5 (E2-17: p* outside the support for 200 ms);
   - TD2C applies to commanded steps only, by design;
   - recovery-step touchdown is unsolved and needs a design decision.
