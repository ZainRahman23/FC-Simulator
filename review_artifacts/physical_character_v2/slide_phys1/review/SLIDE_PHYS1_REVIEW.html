Verdict

REFERENCE MOTION UNDERSTOOD: YES SINGLE-PLAYER PHYSICAL SLIDE: PARTIAL GROUND ENTRY AND SLIDE: PARTIAL PHYSICAL INTERRUPTIBILITY: PARTIAL TWO-PLAYER CONTACT (IF RUN): PARTIAL SLICED-OUT RESPONSE EMERGES WITHOUT SCRIPTING: PARTIAL READY FOR NEXT INTEGRATION MILESTONE: NO RECOMMEND FOR CANONICAL V2: NOT YET

In one paragraph. K is replaced by SP1, a tackler driven only through its own muscles (M1c on the tackler; C3 stays on M1b) and real contacts.

What works:

  • It starts from a joint-consistent running plant-foot touchdown at 4.5–5.5 m/s.
  • It keeps the run's momentum (4.9 m/s) through the plant and a short ballistic flight, then lands on the fold thigh at ≈ 3.7 m/s horizontal and −1.7 m/s vertical.
  • It sits back onto a posting forearm, with no belly-flop, no hover and no phasing.
  • Ledgers close; runs are deterministic.
  • Obstacles fold its tackling leg (up to 131° at the knee) without ever being penetrated (≤ 1.2 mm).
  • Against unchanged C3, the STRONG line produced a sliced-out fall that was not scripted: the near foot was swept 1.5 m along the sweep and left the turf before the pelvis dropped, and the recipient fell along the sweep over the slider.

What doesn't:

  • The tackling heel lands first, as a strut. The tackling ankle inverts 13.5° past its hard limit, and the slide brakes to a stop within ≈ 0.5 m.
  • The slide's pitch balance depends on that leg pressing the turf. Lifting the leg fixes the ankle but the body falls back.
  • A5's whole-body fallback never fired.
  • There is no graded spectrum. LIGHT still delivered 263 N·s, and GRAZE was absorbed only because the slider's boots pressed the recipient's boots down (the PVP-0 statue mechanism).

6 · What needs your decision (stop condition B), and what remains

  1. Slide pitch control. A whole-body (centroidal / seated-balance) controller that does not need the tackling leg to press the turf. This is the same class of problem as BALANCE-3's coordination: a new controller class, not a parameter.
  2. Adopt M1c (athletic shortening force; M1b gives ≈ 0 N·m above 4–5 rad/s) and TCF-1 (studs only when the sole faces the turf) for V2, or not.
  3. Regional, disturbance-relative yield and fallback triggers.

Then: fold knee/thigh before the tackling heel (no strut, no ankle overload); a glide; robustness above 5.25 m/s and at the reference's slower approach; the mirrored side; get-up. Integration: a RUN-1 hand-over module (Q7, Q37); a sim → physics placement and timing layer; recipient protective stepping; resolving the sim/physics slide-length mismatch (≈ 2.3 m vs 0.4–1.1 m); cost at scale. CPU is ≈ 0.75 ms per 240 Hz step per character at runtime, plus 0.21 ms of development-only ledgers.
