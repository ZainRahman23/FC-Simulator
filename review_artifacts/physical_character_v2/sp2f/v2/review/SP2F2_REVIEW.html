1. What REFERENCE3 shows, and what changed

  • REFERENCE3 (as recorded): the slider goes onto his folded side DURING the drop (the trunk already tilted ≈ 30–45° toward the folded side when the hip lands), the folded-side hand reaches the turf at about the hip landing, a long, nearly straight arm out to the side / behind, and the body then slides on that side with the trunk lowering slowly while the arm opens; the other arm is raised (incidental, not copied).
  • Lateral establishment: SP2C's in-air counter-lean is released earlier (lumbar / thoracic lateral flexion toward the folded side over 0.25 → 0.40 s, thoracic-led) so the trunk swings onto the folded side as it lands, then continues along the slow recline (one curve, no pause).
  • Posting arm reach: still moving toward the turf through the contact (0.25 → 0.70 s), targets released 30 ms after the first forearm contact.
  • Active load-aware arm support (new opt-in sp2_ctrl post.support): the press is a two-sided feedback of the sensed trunk lowering (tilt + 0.12 s × rate, 25 ms sensing delay, 40 ms activation) against the SAME intended lowering the trunk targets follow: 130 N on the intended lowering, down to 30 N when the trunk is behind it (yields to the descent), up to 300 N if it runs ahead (brakes a fall). A soft end-range shoulder stabiliser stops the turf dragging the arm overhead (it buckled the elbow at 4.5 m/s).
  • Final organisation: pelvis-roll objective to 70° (SP2F 75°) — robust: with the earlier lateral establishment 75° rolled the body chest-down at 5.5 m/s in nearby variants.
  • Persistent-lateral-orientation diagnostic (pelvis roll, chest normal, trunk roll, shoulder / hip heights, contact regions, classification) and arm effort / load series added to the evaluation.
