1. Why SP2E fell over (5.0 m/s)

  • +0.00…+0.11 s — target discontinuity at contact: SP2E's arm swings from SP2C's back-held pose to the post pose and the fast-start trunk targets move at up to 730°/s of target change → a 300°/s trunk jolt on top of the landing.
  • +0.12 s → +0.57 s — the arm leaves the turf: the hand hits the turf (2 BW) and then the authored arm-OPENING target lifts it 15 cm off the turf (+0.20…+0.57 s).
  • +0.30…+0.60 s — gravity past an unstable threshold: without the post the pelvis rolls over its edge (29° → 82°, roll rate 120°/s) while the authored trunk targets barely change (≤ 38°/s).
  • +0.62 s — the visible event: the torso slams into the turf at 402°/s tipping (−4300°/s²). SP2D has the same structure later: a 133°/s fall at +1.24 s and the hand off the turf at +1.40…+1.65 s.
  • Found on the way: SP2C's in-air counter-lean (trunk toward the tackling side) is what rolls the pelvis onto the folded side for landing (angular-momentum exchange); releasing it in the air lands the pelvis flat (12–15° roll vs 22–31°) and forces the folded hip 9–10° past its external-rotation stop — so the recline starts at contact, not in the air. A pelvis-roll objective at full strength on landing does the same; its strength now builds over 0.35 s.
  • SP2F therefore: (1) one continuous sine-profile recline of every trunk / head target from 0.20 → 1.5 s with the pelvis objective on the same curve (no fast ramp, no phase, no later target or authority step); (2) the arm reaches toward the turf across contact; (3) from the first forearm contact the arm has NO authored angles: its targets fade out and it presses the hand down with 150 N (Jacobian transpose) plus its damping (new opt-in sp2_ctrl opts.post); (4) the pelvis objective's strength builds over 0.35 s after landing.
