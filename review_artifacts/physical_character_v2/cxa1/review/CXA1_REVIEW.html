CXA-1 · do the PVP-1A-DX bodies keep collision exclusion?

Answer: the physical colliders do not phase.
  • Measured: collider penetration stays ≤ 1.2 mm in the committed PVP-1A-DX run, and ≤ 3.3 mm in CF1 (ABI-1's input). That is below Jolt's 5 mm solver slop, with no dropped contact, no crossing and no filtered pair.
  • What you saw is presentation. The tackler is rendered 54–87 mm away from its physics skeleton (Gabriel's rig on a V1-matched body). Both bodies' meshes also extend well beyond their deliberately inscribed colliders, and the boot collider is a sole-only slab.
  • So the rendered footballers interpenetrate by 25–80 mm in every frame, even before any collider touches.
