1. The explicit questions

  1. Can SP2D simply be accelerated? Partly. Compressing SP2D's own schedule (E0–E3) brought "established" from +1.1 s to ≈ +0.5 s but no further: during +0.0…0.3 s the pelvis roll is set by the landing (pelvis on the turf by +0.1 s) and did not respond to a 3–9× pelvis-objective gain or to folded-leg authority 0.35–0.92. Reaching the reference's pace needed the SAME intention started earlier and organised differently: SP2C's tackling-side counter-lean released and the chest opened before contact, then a coordinated trunk lean back / toward the folded side from contact that moves the COM outboard so gravity rolls the pelvis, and a spine settle once lying. The physical slide is not destabilised (never chest-down, no failure, slide within −6…+3 %).
  2. Hand first touch: +0.09 / +0.12 / +0.15 s (4.5 / 5.0 / 5.5 m/s); SP2D +0.49…+0.53 s; reference +0.24…+0.27 s.

The final SP2E commands a slightly LARGER open abduction than SP2D (114° vs 106°) yet peaks lower — the descended torso brings the turf to the hand.

  8. Reference region 130–135°? Approaches it: 4.5 m/s is inside (134°), 5.0 / 5.5 m/s are 3–9° above; the resting arm is below it.

  10. Chest down? Never (all speeds; chest-up component ≥ 0.83 from +0.3 s). The resting posture is more on the back than SP2D's (chest-up 0.88 vs 0.68–0.75; folded side down 0.42–0.46 vs 0.65–0.73).

  12. Actively controlled? Yes — no regional failure, no whole-body fallback; regional authority ≥ 0.54 throughout; the descent is a tracked intention (targets / pelvis objective), not released authority.
  13. Folded-leg tuck damaged? Not at 5.0 / 5.5 m/s (knee in pelvis at contact (−0.12, −0.36, 0.26) vs SP2D (−0.11, −0.37, 0.25); sole-down −0.61…−0.63; studs 0; toe / sole ≈ 0).
