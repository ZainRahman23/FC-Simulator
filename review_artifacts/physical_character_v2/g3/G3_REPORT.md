### 1.2 Run 1 → run 2 (criteria v1 unchanged)

Run 1 evaluated 11/19, before rows O and P were measured. Rows A, B and C failed on a measurement artifact.
- **The artifact:** on the first physics step after release (t = 0.0042 s), the per-piece touch flags lag the contact by one step. Both boots read 0 touching pieces while already carrying 76 N each.
- **Why it hit A–C:** the contact-loss accumulator counted that single tick as "contact loss" in every run.
- **The correction:** the class, contact-loss and unloaded accumulators start at t ≥ 0.5 s. This is the seam-audit window; G2-A10 handled its CoP window the same way.
- **What it did not change:** physics, thresholds and every physics hash (299/299 identical between runs 1 and 2).
- Run 1 measured controller cost 0.168 ms, against 0.152 ms in run 2 for the same code.

Two further corrections, recorded in DECISIONS G3-A11 / A12:
- **Label:** the transverse-plane finding had been filed as "TD-3" in the pre-registration commit. That number already belongs to the passive-layer cost debt, so it is renamed **TD-11**.

## 8. Support geometry and CoP behaviour

- **Support geometry** is the hull of the usable regions of the feet with touching pieces: 524 cm² throughout T5. The opposite foot never stops touching, so it stays in the hull. The **load split** (lever rule along the centroid line) puts ≤ 5 % on it in near-single-support and ≤ 1 % in U.
- **Stance-foot CoP:** in its own frame it sits **4.0 cm ahead of the ankle, centred mediolaterally** (−0.07 cm) through the whole hold. The measured net CoP coincides with the COM projection to < 0.1 mm: a quasi-static hold.
- **Anticipatory postural adjustment (emergent, not scripted):**
  - At ramp onset the net CoP first moves **toward the foot about to be unloaded**: CoP − COM reaches −0.32 cm at t = 1.75–2.0 s (−0.35 cm in U:R).
  - The right foot's load dips to 0.491 before rising.
  - This is the human gait-initiation APA signature. Here it comes from the DCM feed-forward: to accelerate the COM toward the stance foot, the CoP must start on the other side.
- **On the return** the CoP leads toward the centre: 8.15 vs COM 7.83 cm at t = 16 s.
- **Per-foot CoP** changes ≤ 0.65 mm per tick and crosses boot-piece seams 2–116 times per run, smoothly.
- **Heel ↔ forefoot on the stance foot (spec 3.2):** during near-single-support R, the target was moved ±4 cm AP. The stance-foot CoP swept 0.5 → 7.6 cm ahead of the ankle, with load ≥ 0.954 and 0.26 mm slip.
- **Force-plate twin (spec 3.4):** whole-body momentum change equals Σ per-foot contact impulses + gravity (+ test impulse) to ≤ 4.1e-6 of M·g·dt. These are independent computations: per foot from its own momentum and ankle-constraint impulses, versus the whole body.

## 20. Technical debt and open questions

- **G3-F1 / TD-11: transverse-plane compliance at the passive ankle ab/adduction (decision needed before G4).**
  - **The cause:**
    - The axis is passive-only by approved anatomy, and the G1 passive tissue is end-range-only: **0 N·m inside ±10°**, 3.4 N·m at 12°, 10 N·m at the 15° hard limit.
    - No actuator spans it: the ankle's DF and inversion axes are horizontal for a flat foot.
    - So the legs twist freely on the planted feet until the tissue engages.
  - **What it causes:**
    - 10.5–11.1° in every slow transfer; 12.3–13.1° in fast transfers and pushes; 12.9° in T4.
    - The hip rotators counter-rotate ≈ 10° to hold the pelvis (yaw ≤ 2.1° in slow transfers, 6.6–8.6° in fast ones or under pushes).
  - **Static whole-body yaw stiffness:** 2.8 N·m/° bilateral, 0.9–1.1 N·m/° near-single-support.
  - **It predates G3:** the accepted G2 controller shows 10.5–13.6° under bilateral pushes.
  - **Not changed** (approved anatomy / passive tissue / actuation). Options:
    - (a) load-dependent neutral-zone stiffness of the ankle's axial rotation (the mortise is stiff when loaded);
    - (b) a small foot ab/adduction actuator;
    - (c) a 2-DOF talocrural/subtalar ankle without free axial play.
  - **Why it matters for G4:** single support in a step loads this axis far more.
- **Cross-engine determinism of the standing controller (row O).** `Math.hypot` / `Math.atan2` in physics-feeding controller code: G2-era IK, heading, allocation and polygon distance, plus G3's ikFeasible. The fix is confirmed: use the project's `dlen` / `datan2`, or `sqrt` of a sum of squares. It changes the accepted G2 hashes at the last-bit level, so it needs a G2 re-validation and a G3 run 3. **Recommended before G4.**
- **Near-single-support steady-state error:** 0.97 maps to 0.963–0.968. A load closed loop would remove it (row I).
- **Minimality (§3):** remove contactSupport / holdUnloaded, or keep them as G4 guards.
- **Mirror tolerance for sliding contact (row J):** the plant's own sliding asymmetry (≤ 1.5 mm at G2) exceeds the tolerance I set.
- **Controller cost under contention (row S):** IK warm start or fewer Newton iterations, with no behaviour change.
- **Carried over:** TD-1 (extended), TD-2, TD-3 (passive-layer cost), TD-4…TD-10 unchanged. TD-8 (engineering constants) gains the G3 constants: loadOff / loadOn 1 / 3 % BW; supervisor 0.85 / 1 cm / 20 ms / 0.6 s; hold-window offset 0.5 s.
