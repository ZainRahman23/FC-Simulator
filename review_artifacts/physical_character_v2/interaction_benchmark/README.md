# IB-1: interaction benchmark (frozen 10 Oct 2026)

**Status:**
- Frozen as test material during the architecture review. Source: `../sources/2026-10-10_user_instruction_pause_consolidate.md`.
- It does not choose, implement or favour an architecture.
- Nothing in the simulation, presentation, V2, Jolt settings, collision geometry or any frozen criterion was changed to make it.

**Purpose:** a future interaction system is swapped in underneath the same authoritative football events. It is then measured against them with `METRICS.md`.

**What is authoritative:** only the simulation export, i.e. the V1.3 **OFF** record of each case.

**What is optional:** the presentation pose streams (V1.3 LOCO, LC-1 LOCO). They are non-authoritative inputs, and they have known defects (see `../consolidation/CANONICAL_EVIDENCE.md`).

## 2. What each scenario file contains

`scenarios/<id>_<case>.json`, generated by `scripts/ib_extract.mjs`:

- **Provenance:**
  - simulation commit `5042230` (`prototype/slide-contact-v1.3-charcollide`, local);
  - lineage: V1.2 e2c98ec + CHARCOLLIDE-1 profile 3e28e02 (profile SHA in the file);
  - exporter `../pi1/trackB/scripts/air_export_v13.cjs`, schema `pi1.air/3-v13`, 260 ticks;
  - every record's path, bytes, SHA-256, content hash without `cpu`, and recomputed gameplay hash;
  - the committed export summaries those records are checked against.
- **Authoritative inputs:**
  - the fixture params;
  - the drill and command script, verbatim (`scenario.drill`, `scenario.cmds`);
  - characters (runner `vinicius` 73 kg, tackler `gabriel` 78 kg);
  - `legLen` / `LEG_REF` 0.865 m;
  - the simulation's slide and reaction constants (`PT_DEF.slide`, `PT_REACT`), verbatim.
- **Authoritative outcome:**
  - gameplay hash and final class;
  - every event verbatim (TACKLE_START, TACKLE_BODY_CONTACT, PLAYER_CONTACT with its contact-model fields, PLAYER_CONTACT_ABSORBED, TACKLE, …);
  - the reaction timeline and every reaction state verbatim. For FALL: t0, tGround, tStop, tRec, tUp, azimuth, family, spin.
- **Per contact:**
  - the event (point, normal, penetration, vn / vt, J, dvCom, spin, dvTackler, landShift, steps, until, support, stride, gait phase);
  - its row / slot / τ, and whether it is the decisive contact;
  - the tackler primitives and runner capsules at that sub-step, verbatim;
  - derived surface distances (pairs within 50 mm);
  - the tackler slide state;
  - kinematics in the pre-contact row and the contact row;
  - support state: the authoritative event support / stride / segPlanted, `simBody` leg planted / up, and phase. The presentation foot-contact flags are also given, labelled non-authoritative.
- **Near miss / overlap:**
  - the recorded minimum d_now;
  - the derived closest pair;
  - the first geometric overlap. In all four contact cases it coincides with the gameplay contact sub-step.
- **Kinematics window:** runner and tackler x, y, vx, vy, facing and gait phase, plus the reaction kind and the recorded d_now / d_pred, for every row from the predictor row − 12 to the end of the reaction.
- **Existing evidence:**
  - the V1.3 compatibility-gate criteria, verbatim;
  - the REV2 scan verdict.
  - The PI-1 predictor row is given, labelled architecture-specific.

## 4. Conventions

- **Time:**
  - Row k is the authoritative state after squad tick k+1 (60 Hz).
  - Event (tick T, sub n) ↔ row T−1, sub-step slot n−1, τ = T − 1 + n/4. Row r's end state is τ = r + 1.
  - The exporter's summary field `firstPredTick` is a row index.
- **Row columns:**
  - 0 = row;
  - 1 – 5 = ball x, y, z, vx, vy;
  - 6 = owner;
  - 7 = active;
  - 8 – 13 = runner x, y, vx, vy, facing, gaitPhase;
  - 14 – 19 = tackler (same layout).
- **Units:** m, m/s, rad, kg, N·s. Pitch plane x, y; z up.
- **Gameplay hash:** FNV-1a of JSON(rows) + "|" + JSON(events), the exporter's `hashOf`.
- **Contact:**
  - **The gameplay contact is the `PLAYER_CONTACT` event, not geometric overlap.**
  - `J` and `dvCom` / `spin` / `dvTackler` are the simulation's own contact-model values. They are authoritative over the football outcome. They are not physical ground truth.

**What the records do not contain** (not to be invented by a consumer):
- **No physical ground truth:** no forces, no ground-reaction forces, no angular momentum and no inertia of either player beyond the masses.
- **No full authoritative runner skeleton.** The simulation has only its collision capsules from the shared leg law (`simBody`). Full poses exist only in the presentation streams.
- **The tackler** is the simulation's slide primitives with per-primitive velocities, not a body.
- **No contact patch** beyond `point`, `normal` and penetration.
- **No authoritative recovery pose.**

## 5. How a future interaction system consumes it

**Inputs, read-only:**
- the rows;
- the per-sub-step runner capsules (`simBody`) and tackler primitives (`prims`);
- the events and reaction states;
- optionally one presentation stream (`pres`, `bones`, `bind`) from either LOCO record.

**It must not:** write to any of them, read beyond the current tick (PI-1 DT-5 style), or alter the outcome.

**Procedure:**
1. Run `node interaction_benchmark/scripts/ib_verify.mjs` before and after.
2. Evaluate against `METRICS.md`. Hard requirements H1 – H8 are pass/fail; quality metrics Q1 – Q8 are reported.

**Regeneration** (not needed to consume):
- V1.3: `../pi1/trackB/scripts/air_export_v13.cjs`, run against a worktree at `5042230` serving `sandbox/visual/match.html` (usage in the script header).
- LC-1: `../locomotion_continuity/scripts/lc_export.cjs` on `prototype/locomotion-continuity-v1` (9d57d46).
- OFF records are bit-reproducible (`../pi1/trackB/v13/air/air_summary_repeat.json`). LOCO / FULL file bytes are not (`cpu`); compare them by gameplay hash and by the content hash without `cpu`.
