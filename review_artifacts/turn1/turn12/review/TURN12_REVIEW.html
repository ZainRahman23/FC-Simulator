Mirroring

Exact in all 28 pairs (a right turn started on the right foot against a left turn started on the left foot): COM 0 mm, events identical, layer channels exact. Mirrored joints differ by ≤ 26.8 mm, the same as TURN-1's own control (≤ 26.8 mm, RUN-1.3's arm asymmetry).

The resolver (exact logic)

  • CURVE: the requested change is within TURN-1's arc limit (50° at 3 m/s → 25° at 9.5 m/s).
  • Brake family: taken only if both hold — the angle is large for the speed (≥ aLarge − 10°, where aLarge is 118° at 5 m/s, 110° at 6.5, 105° at 8 and 102° at 9.5), and TURN-1 would still have to remove ≥ 3.6 m/s before its plant (s − sEntry(angle)). Inside aLarge ± 10° the stride state decides: TURN-1's own plan for this request is evaluated, and its braking-stance count nB (it depends on which foot is next) keeps TURN-1 unless nB + 3x ≥ 4, where x runs from 0 to 1 across the band.
  • BRAKE_SHUFFLE_TURN vs BRAKE_TURN: shuffle steps when the base rotation the turn plant cannot do (angle − 105°) is ≥ 20°, or speed remains to remove. Their count is that rotation at ≤ 50° per step plus the speed left. Once the base is open the turn plant must be the outside foot: one braking stance fewer, or one shuffle step more, makes it so.
  • Otherwise: TURN-1's own cut or pivot (TURN1_CUT / TURN1_PIVOT), unchanged — except that a TURN-1 pivot plants on the next touchdown once a braking stance has brought its speed within 0.6 m/s of the pivot's entry speed (compact).
