Measurements, every maneuver (TURN-1 → TURN-1.1)

How each column is measured:

  • Reaction: the first instant after the request at which the head, hands or elbows (relative to the pelvis) differ by ≥ 8 cm from the same player given no command.
  • Lean: the torso (pelvis → neck) toward the turn, over the plant. Back: the largest back-lean.
  • Deep: the time with the pelvis ≥ 15 cm below RUN-1's at that speed.
  • Sitting: the time with the pelvis ≥ 12 cm low and the torso within 15° of upright.
  • Shoulders: the shoulder line against the hip line (it includes RUN-1's counter-rotation).
  • Span: the hand-to-hand horizontal span.
  • Free knee: its height at the plant touchdown.
  • Slip: the worst loaded stance.
  • Pelvis: the largest 240 Hz residual.
