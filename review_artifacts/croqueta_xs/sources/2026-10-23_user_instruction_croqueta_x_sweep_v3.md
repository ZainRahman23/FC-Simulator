# THE CURRENT FUNDAMENTAL ERROR

Right now the movement still reads approximately as:

```text
BALL ON RIGHT
→ sharp lateral transfer / cut
→ BALL ON LEFT
```

Even though we now have the hop, this is still not the spatial/body geometry of the Croqueta I want.

The Croqueta should feel like **one continuous sweeping motion from the instant it begins**.

The ball, legs, hips and torso should trace a much more curved/diagonal relationship through the action.

# THINK OF THE BODY AS FORMING TWO HALVES OF AN X

This is conceptual, not literal geometry.

During the RIGHT-side portion of the movement:

```text
RIGHT LEG / BALL SIDE extends outward-right and forward

RIGHT HIP / RIGHT TORSO bends and loads toward the RIGHT

LEFT LEG trails behind

the body's right side becomes concave / loaded
```

Then the entire geometry sweeps through center.

During the LEFT-side portion:

```text
LEFT LEG / BALL SIDE extends outward-left and forward

LEFT HIP / LEFT TORSO bends and loads toward the LEFT

RIGHT LEG now trails behind

the body's left side becomes concave / loaded
```

So the action has a visual transformation approximately like:

```text
NORMAL UPRIGHT DRIBBLE

→ RIGHT-SIDE BOW / X-HALF

→ SWEEP THROUGH CENTER

→ LEFT-SIDE BOW / X-HALF

→ EXIT
```

The body should not remain vertically stacked over relatively straight legs.
