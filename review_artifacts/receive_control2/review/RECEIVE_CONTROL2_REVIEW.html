Known limitations

  • Moving receivers still use RECEIVE-CONTROL-1.1's action geometry (STRIDE / OPEN_TURN / THROUGH_TURN) — the interim until QUEUE #2; their execution is the unified model.
  • RECEIVE-TURN-1's actions are authored for a frontal ball: a standing receiver squares up to the pass before a turn (over 0.5 s); kills and controls keep his facing (the bearing is a difficulty term).
  • After a kill or control with a go intent (A2, I2) the receiver turns and pushes off from RECEIVE-KILL's standing body; the run out of the set-off is RECEIVE-KILL's body sliding into TURN-1.2's first strides — a visible placeholder.
  • DRIBBLE-V1's frozen stride-phase pocket: 1 in 90 integrated go receives (and RECEIVE-CONTROL-1.1's moving TAKEN cases) is a ball on the line that DRIBBLE-V1 does not touch.
  • No Agility, no Dribbling attribute, no pressure.
