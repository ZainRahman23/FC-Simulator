What changed

The local shot setup corrects the inherited inside-foot spin-axis sign at contact: −Y → +Y. With forward +X velocity, positive Y produces inward Magnus toward −Z. No camera mirror or foot swap is used. The existing Curve magnitude stays exactly 40 × (Curve / 75)^2.7 rad/s. The original resolver, previous evidence and all frozen sources remain unchanged; this review applies the sign correction in its own pre-contact wrapper.

Launch yaw and elevation are found by a physical two-variable solve at the same tolerance for every rating. One launch is made. There is no target seeking, late multiplier, trajectory spline, teleport or post-launch spin correction. Spin subsequently decays through the unchanged physical world.
