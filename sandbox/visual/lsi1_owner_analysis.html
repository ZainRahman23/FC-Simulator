The owner's own left-stick recordings from LEFT-STICK INPUT LAB V1, analysed offline. This is design evidence, not training data: no recognizer exists, nothing here runs live, and no gameplay file is changed. Every angle is player-relative, measured from the line the player was running (+ = his right).

READ THE DATA WITH THESE LIMITS

1 · Mouse, not thumb. All 21 attempts were made with a mouse dragging the on-screen stick. The pointer travelled up to ~8 stick radii from the centre, so the stick sat at full tilt almost throughout: magnitude carried no information, and samples arrive once per frame (60 Hz).

2 · The left screen edge. . Every leftward swing that went further than that stopped there, and the stick's direction then followed only the pointer's height. Left-side angles are set by the edge, not by the hand (14 of the 20 direction gestures). The lab now has pad at centre / ?pad=centre for the next recordings.

3 · No G marks. Each gesture's start is inferred: the last frame still on the held running line before a change of 15° or more. All times are measured from that inferred onset.

4 · One context. Every direction gesture started from the same state: a straight run toward the coaches at 5.3 m/s (RUN), ball on the right foot, steady carry. The sandbox player is right-footed. There is no walk or jog evidence and no left-foot evidence.

FINDINGS IN ONE SCREEN

BODY FEINT · 7

All 7 are a contradiction: a full-tilt swing to one side of the running line, a hold of 50–120 ms, then a swing across the line to the other side, held until release. The exit side is reached 167–250 ms after the start. The route between the sides varied: through the centre twice, around the back once, across the front four times. The signature is the side contradiction plus its timing, not a path or an angle.

CROQUETA · 5

All 5 are also contradictions: a smaller swing against the croqueta side (26–45°) with a longer hold (67–250 ms), then the croqueta side. That is the deception shape at lower amplitude, and the ranges meet the feint's (45° vs 48°). There is no croqueta-specific gesture. No attempt could have produced a canonical Croqueta: the player was at a run, and Croqueta V4 is validated from a jog (2.6–3.6 m/s); to the right it is the left-footed twin's move.

ORDINARY TURNS · 8

All 8 sharp and gentle turns are one-sided: none went 10° or more to the side opposite their exit. Angular velocity does not separate them from feints (sharp cuts reached 686–1545°/s; one feint peaked at 1568°/s). The side contradiction does. Not yet recorded: zig-zag, "changed my mind", 180° reversal, normal carry.

WHEN THE BODY COULD DO IT

For the shape this player can execute (fake left → exit right), the canonical Body Feint requested at the inferred onset plays 8 of 8 times. Its first stance comes +179–592 ms after onset, quantized by the stride (one step = 316 ms at a run). Requested at the recognition point (+167–362 ms), it plays 6 of 8 as played and 8 of 8 if steering is held through the first phase (counterfactual). The owner's own exit angles (67–82°) lie outside the run band (35–40°).

THE SHAPES — EVERY GESTURE

Each point is one real attempt, mirrored so its exit is to the right; hover for details. The counter is the first phase's peak against the exit side; 0 means one-sided. Hollow points ran into the left screen edge, so their left-side angle is a floor set by the edge, not a measurement.

PHYSICAL OPPORTUNITY — THE CANONICAL GATES, ASKED AT EVERY FRAME

Each owner session is re-simulated exactly (o0 + the recorded input), the sandbox's own explicit request (the 7 / 8 / 9 / 0 keys) is issued at a chosen frame, and the canonical resolver's answer is read back. AS PLAYED = the owner's input as recorded, then the request. HELD = counterfactual: the movement input held on the running line from the onset, as if a recognizer had not steered the first phase. The recognition point is the first frame on the exit side (10° or more past the line), or the settle for one-sided changes. All times are ms from the inferred onset.
